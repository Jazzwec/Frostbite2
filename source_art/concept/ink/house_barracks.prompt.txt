Palette: Falu red #B2412E, ochre #C9973F, faded teal #5E8A87, snow #EEF2F2, blue shadow #7F93A6, deep ink #2C3440, firelight #F2B266. Designed like a real indie game's art style with deliberate simplification and restraint: few materials, large clean colour areas, no surface texture noise, no rendered glow or bloom, no sparkles, no glossy plastic, no generic 3D animated-movie look, no over-detailed props, no text, no labels, no UI.
