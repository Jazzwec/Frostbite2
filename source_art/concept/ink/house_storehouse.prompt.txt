Rendered in INK AND WATERCOLOUR PICTURE-BOOK style: hand-inked slightly wobbly dark outlines, soft watercolour colour washes with visible paper grain, gently simplified shapes, like a Nordic children's picture book brought into a 3D game with toon shading and outlines. Palette: Falu red #B2412E, ochre #C9973F, faded teal #5E8A87, snow #EEF2F2, blue shadow #7F93A6, deep ink #2C3440, firelight #F2B266. Designed like a real indie game's art style with deliberate simplification and restraint: few materials, large clean colour areas, no surface texture noise, no rendered glow or bloom, no sparkles, no glossy plastic, no generic 3D animated-movie look, no over-detailed props, no text, no labels, no UI. SUBJECT: the village STOREHOUSE, a single chunky Nordic timber storehouse building for a mobile game, gable end facing the viewer. Ochre-painted vertical timber walls (one flat ochre colour mass with a few broad darker battens), dark brown corner posts, a low grey fieldstone base of a few large stones. A steep gable roof completely covered by a thick soft rounded snow cap that hangs over the eaves in a few broad lumps, with the dark wooden roof edge just showing under the snow. Wide double doors in Falu red with a light wood frame, one simple stone step. Above the doors a hanging wooden sign with a simple painted crooked-sun motif. Two stacked wooden crates with snow on top against the left wall. Proportions: about 3.7 m wide, 2.6 m deep, walls 2.5 m tall plus the roof; walls clearly visible, the roof not overwhelming them.
