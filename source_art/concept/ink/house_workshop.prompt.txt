Rendered in INK AND WATERCOLOUR PICTURE-BOOK style: hand-inked slightly wobbly dark outlines, soft watercolour colour washes with visible paper grain, gently simplified shapes, like a Nordic children's picture book brought into a 3D game with toon shading and outlines. Palette: Falu red #B2412E, ochre #C9973F, faded teal #5E8A87, snow #EEF2F2, blue shadow #7F93A6, deep ink #2C3440, firelight #F2B266. Designed like a real indie game's art style with deliberate simplification and restraint: few materials, large clean colour areas, no surface texture noise, no rendered glow or bloom, no sparkles, no glossy plastic, no generic 3D animated-movie look, no over-detailed props, no text, no labels, no UI. SUBJECT: the WORKSHOP: a natural brown timber workshop with a deep-teal door, a fieldstone chimney on one side of the roof, a hanging sign with a simple painted hammer motif, a chunky iron anvil on a stump and a crate beside the door. About 3 m wide, 2.6 m deep. Same building family as the attached storehouse: vertical timber walls in one flat colour mass with a few broad darker battens, dark brown corner posts, a low base of a few large grey fieldstones, a steep gable roof completely covered by a thick soft rounded snow cap hanging over the eaves in a few broad lumps with the dark wooden roof edge just showing, gable end facing the viewer, walls clearly visible and the roof not overwhelming them.
